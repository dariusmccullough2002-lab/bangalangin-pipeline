    THE TWO-SIDED VIEW

    What changes for each owner?

    Incoming minus outgoing estimated annual production. Rates are package ratios—not the change in your entire team's lineup. Prospects without MLB stats and picks have no fabricated MLB category lines.

    How values are calculated & what remains uncertain

    All 12 categories have equal league weight. Category units use initial divisors: HR 8, R/RBI 25, SB 8, K 45, ER −15, SVH7 30, QA3 5. AVG uses excess hits above .250 / 15; OPS uses excess OPS above .720 × AB / 40; K/BB uses excess K above 3 × BB / 40; WHIP uses excess prevention below 1.25 / 15. These are transparent, uncalibrated scaling choices. Production is 12 + 4 × summed units, capped at 0–100 when playing time exists. No-season-stat players do not receive that floor.

    Year weights: Contender 65/25/10%, Balanced 40/30/30%, Rebuild 15/25/60%. Prospect/pick multipliers: .85/1/1.15. Young-player rates rise modestly; rates and workload decline with age. These are coarse scenarios rather than a fitted aging model. Season-only baselines may understate injured players and overstate career years. Batting raw counts are aggregated before calculating AVG and OPS; pitching innings are converted from baseball notation.

    Prospect value uses the existing Pipeline rank and ETA, with a risk discount. It takes the larger of prospect value and production value to avoid adding the same upside twice. Pick round bases are 48/29/18/11/7, discounted 15% per future year; early/late estimates vary ±20%. Slots are unfinalized. No confirmed 2029 transfers. A starting eight-point roster-space cost per net incoming player models consolidation; edit it below.

    Contracts affect role scenarios and uncertainty; MLB salary is not a fantasy salary charge. Research links are starting points, not evidence that every linked player has been researched. Manual settings apply to both owners' valuations of that player and remain in this browser until reset.

    QA3: ≥5 IP/≤2 ER or ≥6 IP/≤3 ER. SVH7 = 3SV + 2HLD. K/BB = K ÷ BB; zero walks uses 1.01 × K. Weekly 35-IP minimum is enforced by Fantrax; annual package IP cannot establish whether a roster will meet it each week.
