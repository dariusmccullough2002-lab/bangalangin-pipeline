# CPI+ — Contextual Pitching Index

CPI+ replaces opposition difficulty as the main pitcher score on the AFL page, canonical player cards and the pitcher leaderboard at #afl/cpi. The old #afl/pitcher-oqi route remains an alias. Independent batter quality remains a supporting adjustment, visible in the table; its algorithm and circularity safeguards are unchanged. Regular-season and postseason data remain combined; Fall Stars excluded.

## Formula

Use complete active AFL PitchIQ records and official combined pitching statistics. Within the pitch-quality block, standardize Filth+ (60%), whiff% (20%), CSW% (12%) and chase% (8%). Filth+ is available in the AFL feed; no distinct Stuff+ or TiltStuff+ field is exposed there, so no grade is invented or relabeled. The related pitch measures share a single block and are not claimed to be independent signals. PitchIQ's own source describes Filth+ as a physical pitch-based bat-missing model: https://pitchiq.prospecttilt.com/aging-methodology . This is source documentation, not independent validation of CPI+.

Final base blend: 65% pitch quality, 25% official K−BB%, 10% inverse ERA. K−BB% appears once explicitly, from official K/BB/BF, not again in pitch quality. ERA is ER×27/outs. Standardize inputs against the qualified current cohort and cap individual z values to ±3. Regress the quality block by pitches/(pitches+150), K−BB% by BF/(BF+40), and the ERA block by outs/(outs+45), equivalent to IP/(IP+15). Multiply the blended standardized result by 15.

Add context points = clamp((independent batter-quality OQI−100)×0.20, −3, +3)×matched BF/(matched BF+40). Better opposition modestly helps; worse opposition modestly hurts. This is a bounded context term, never the primary pitcher rating. Remove all own outcomes from hitter, team and league offensive references as in the prior OQI implementation; no CPI+ or CBS+ feedback. A pitcher’s CPI+ should improve with better own results, while independent opposition OQI stays fixed. Center the qualified CPI+ cohort at 100. The centering constant is a display baseline and never feeds opponent quality.

Weights, shrinkage constants, clipping and adjustment limits are editorial design choices, not fitted/validated MLB forecasting coefficients. Default qualification is 20 official BF and 50 tracked pitches. All complete small samples still receive scores and can be shown; incomplete inputs show a dash, not a fake league-average score. Current data provide full scores for 157 pitchers, 148 qualified. Source timestamps remain visible because pitch-quality data may lag official results.

## Integration and evidence

The shared daily AFL refresh generates CPI+, independent OQI, hitter CBS+ and combined saved stats together. It saves the exact active-pitcher physical-metric subset to data/afl/cpi-pitch-inputs-2026.json for reproducibility. It does not add provider K/BB or provider ERA a second time. Official counts supply the results components. The existing hitter CBS+ outputs and independent pitcher OQI are unchanged with the same input snapshot.

40 focused tests pass. They check directionality for quality/K/BB/ERA, no double counting of provider K/BB/ERA, bounded context, sample regression, neutral/missing handling, UI sort/search and prior AFL behavior. Real-data audit: 307 better-result direction checks pass with no opposition-component change. Separate 314 full-game mutation checks across all 157 pitchers prove that their independent OQI is unchanged when every own outcome is replaced by a strikeout or home run. Both audit JSONs are committed. The unrelated pre-existing ProspectRank verification-registry test remains outside this change.

Production build passes. Exact inputs and displayed component contributions permit recomputation; see scripts/audit-cpi.mjs and docs/website/cpi-audit-20261112.json.
